Dependency shortlist and licences

Every dependency is checked by cargo deny check licenses against deny.toml. Apache-2.0-only, GPL, LGPL, AGPL and other copyleft licences are rejected. Dual MIT OR Apache-2.0 crates pass through their MIT arm.

Transitive dependencies of the above (num-traits, memchr, termcolor, the toml family, windows-sys, unicode-ident and others) were all checked by cargo metadata and are MIT, MIT OR Apache-2.0, Unlicense OR MIT, or (MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND Unicode-3.0.

Not added: Criterion and Divan (planned as dev-only comparison benchmarks, still to do).

Verify at any time with:

cargo deny check licenses
